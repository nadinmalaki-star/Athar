## Known sourcing gap

I could not find a freely-licensed photo showing a **reception/check-in
moment with a visible patient** that didn't also carry a real clinic's own
logo prominently in frame (the best candidates on both Unsplash and Pexels
for "patient at a dental reception desk" came from the same real clinic's
photo shoot and show that clinic's actual signage/wordmark). Using those
would put a real business's branding inside VERA's fictional demo, so I did
not use them. Rather than force a lower-quality substitute, I kept the
existing, already-clean VERA V2 reception photo (no third-party branding,
good quality, just no person in frame) for that slot. If a better option is
available via a paid stock library or a custom shoot, that would be the
fix — flagging this as the one specific blocker per the brief's own
instruction, not papering over it.

## Minor known imperfection

`consultation.jpg` (8669944) shows clinic staff in uniform with a small
embroidered badge/crest on the scrub top. It's a real clinic's branding,
but small and not legible at the sizes used on the page. Noting it for
transparency rather than claiming a fully clean source.
