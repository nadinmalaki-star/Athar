Meet the team

Dr. Lina Haddad

Lead Dentist & Founder, VERA Clinic (fictional)

Dr. Haddad founded VERA Clinic around a simple idea: dental visits should be calm, clearly explained, and built around the patient's time — not the other way around. She leads a small team of general and cosmetic dentists supported by a dedicated patient-care coordinator.

Every first visit starts with a conversation, not a form — so treatment plans are understood before they're agreed to.

Dr. Lina Haddad and VERA Clinic are fictional, created for this ATHAR Digital Studio demonstration. No real clinic, practitioner, or patient outcome is represented.
